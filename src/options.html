Connection

The extension connects to the browser listener of your coddy serve and lets its chats use the tabs you share. Turn the listener on with browser.enable in Coddy's configuration.

The name tells this browser apart when several are connected to one server.

Always allowed websites

Coddy acts on these sites without asking. Every other site asks first.

    How sharing works

    Coddy reaches only the tabs in its tab groups. Share a tab from the toolbar button, or drag it into the Coddy group. A chat that works in a shared tab moves it into a group of its own, named after the chat; a tab Coddy opens starts there. Drag a tab out of a group to take it back.

    Stop Coddy cancels every action and ungroups every tab; the tabs stay open.
