Connection

The extension connects to your coddy serve, shows its web UI in the side panel and lets its chats use the tabs you share. Turn it on with browser.enable in Coddy's configuration.

The name tells this browser apart when several are connected to one server.

Always allowed websites

The chats of your server act on these sites without asking; every other site asks first. This machine's addresses are on the list from the start, on any port but your Coddy server's own. A chat from another machine asks about every site.

    How sharing works

    Coddy reaches only the tabs in its tab groups. Share a tab with Share in the side panel, or drag it into the Coddy group. A chat that works in a shared tab moves it into a group of its own, named after the chat; a tab Coddy opens starts there. Drag a tab out of a group, or press Take back, to take it back.

    A chat of another Coddy, such as a console or a node of your swarm, asks you before its first action, and its tab group names the machine it runs on.

    Disconnect cancels every action, ungroups every tab and takes back what the chats from other machines were allowed; the tabs stay open. The Stop button on a page Coddy works in does the same and keeps the connection.
